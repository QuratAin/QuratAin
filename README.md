## ✦ About Me

I'm a frontend engineer with **4 years of experience** building and maintaining production Shopify storefronts.

My work sits at the intersection of **frontend engineering, Shopify's platform, ecommerce UX, and real business requirements**. I enjoy taking complex storefront problems and turning them into clean, maintainable experiences for both merchants and customers.

I've worked across growing multi-vendor marketplaces, custom Shopify themes, international storefronts, Theme App Extensions, complex product catalogs, and app-to-theme integrations.

## ✦ Selected Work

### Multi-Vendor & International Storefronts

Worked on production Shopify storefronts supporting large product catalogs, multiple Shopify Markets, custom navigation, collection filtering, variant experiences, campaign landing pages, and ongoing storefront development.

### Theme App Extensions

Built storefront components that connected Shopify themes with application data, using **Liquid, JavaScript, REST APIs, GraphQL, webhooks, Node.js/Express, and PostgreSQL** where required by the frontend experience.

### Product & Variant Architecture

Worked on storefronts where separately managed products needed to be consolidated into cleaner variant structures while preserving **SKUs, inventory dependencies, reviews, bundles, and the existing customer experience**.

### Multilingual & RTL Development

Developed and customized international Shopify storefront experiences, including **English/Arabic interfaces and RTL support**.
